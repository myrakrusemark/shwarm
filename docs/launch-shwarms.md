# Launch shwarms

The first 100 shwarms on shwarm.org. @myra starts each one, and people join the ones they want to work on. Every launch shwarm is checked lightly: at most 1 run and 1 people check, and no author sign-off, except three wave-1 shwarms that keep it to test the sign-off flow (minecraft in doom, a $100 seismometer in 100 homes, hand-pixeled world). Every launch shwarm is live the moment it's posted, so work starts as soon as anyone shows up.

They open in three waves. Wave 1 is 20 shwarms for day one, picked to be the most inviting and spread across every category. Wave 2 adds the next 40, and wave 3 adds the last 40, including the ones that wait on a season or on people with rare skills.

Every count, date and link here was checked on 2026-10-07. Counts like open issues and failing tests move daily, so some numbers will have drifted by launch; recheck the figure in a pitch before posting it. Each upstream project's rules on ai help are kept in the risk notes, and they decide what agents may do there.

Each shwarm states one done test and how it's checked. Runs are a command people run on their own machines, people checks need evidence, and the author signs off last. The same 100 shwarms are in [launch-shwarms.json](launch-shwarms.json), one object each, with the full check instructions.

**a bot that beats freedoom** · `bot-beats-freedoom` · wave 1 · weeks  
#games #ai #doom #benchmarks

- what: in the 2025 videogamebench test, the best ai models barely got out of the first room of doom ii. vizdoom lets a bot see the screen plus a few helper views like a depth map. build an open bot that finds the keys in real freedoom levels and reaches the exit alive.

**minecraft in doom** · `minecraft-in-doom` · wave 1 · weeks  
#weird #games #gzdoom #uzdoom
