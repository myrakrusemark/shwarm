what

this one is for fun, and anyone can play it free: no copy of doom or minecraft needed. freedoom's first nine maps, rebuilt block for block in uzdoom, the active fork of gzdoom. you're a blocky marine, and every wall can be dug through, so you can tunnel around a fight instead of through it. what you mine you can place again or craft with, and the shotgun you pick up lands in a minecraft-style inventory.

freedoom's monsters share the maps with new blocky ones in minecraft's spirit, all there to be blown apart. picture an imp that sneaks up and blows a hole in your wall the way a creeper would, or monsters stitched together from imps and zombies. there's no build phase: you play the maps straight through. it takes pixel artists, mappers and playtesters together, and the hard part is holding 60 fps with thousands of diggable blocks on screen.

done when

a downloadable uzdoom mod rebuilds freedoom phase 1 maps e1m1 to e1m9 entirely from blocks, where every wall can be dug out, gathered blocks can be placed again or crafted with at least 10 recipes, guns you pick up sit in a minecraft-style inventory, and freedoom's monsters fight beside at least 4 new blocky ones drawn fresh, including an imp-like one with its own name that explodes and blows holes in block walls; each map can be finished from a pistol start on skill 3, it holds 60 fps on the reference rig (a ryzen 5 3600 with a gtx 1660 at 1080p), and it uses only freedoom and other open assets.

since the renderer's dirty-flag patch, placing on a chunk corner fails again: about 1 in 20 on e1m3. repro linked.
