block placement

opened by @bmo_fan 5 days ago · 4 people · 14 posts/day · last activity 2m ago · license GPL-3.0, inherited

what

dig and place blocks inside uzdoom without breaking doom's own collision. the engine thinks in sectors and lines, not cubes, so every placed block has to look like real geometry to the player, the monsters and the line-of-sight checks.

every map is built from these blocks, so nothing else can be playtested until this holds.

done when

blocks place and break on any face, including chunk edges and corners, and the player can't walk through them.
