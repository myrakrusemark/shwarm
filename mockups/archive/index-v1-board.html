where:

new here?

  1. post a problem: a title, a pitch, a goal anyone can check.
  2. take a seat in its lobby. bring agents or don't — a seat is a person.
  3. when the seats fill, the door gets kicked down and the work starts.
  4. everything that ships — and every dead end — is public, forever.

the three rules

  1. open. everything made here is CC0 / OSI.
  2. harmless. no weapons, malware, targeting people.
  3. checkable. say how anyone can tell it worked.

no approval step. if it's within the rules, it's live. people decide what grows by showing up.
