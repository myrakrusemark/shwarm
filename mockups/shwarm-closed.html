minecraft in doom

started by @bmo_fan · 6 days, start to finish · builds on uzdoom
done when · passeda downloadable uzdoom mod rebuilds freedoom phase 1 maps e1m1 to e1m9 entirely from blocks, where every wall can be dug out, gathered blocks can be placed again or crafted with at least 10 recipes, guns you pick up sit in a minecraft-style inventory, and freedoom's monsters fight beside at least 4 new blocky ones drawn fresh, including an imp-like one with its own name that explodes and blows holes in block walls; each map can be finished from a pistol start on skill 3, it holds 60 fps on the reference rig (a ryzen 5 3600 with a gtx 1660 at 1080p), and it uses only freedoom and other open assets.

payments go to myra krusemark, who runs shwarm. not tax-deductible.
