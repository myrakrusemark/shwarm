go-live tracker · proofread and ideas

drag a card to another column or up and down inside one. the buttons on each card do the same: ↑ ↓ move it, 1 2 3 send it to a wave, × moves it to "out".

top of a column goes first. the order saves on every move, and claude reads it from tracker/waves.json. "out" holds the cards you dropped; drag one back if you change your mind.
